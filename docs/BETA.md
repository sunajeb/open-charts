## open-charts beta: for test users

Thank you for trying open-charts. This is a **test version**. By installing it you accept that:

- it is **not signed** yet, so your Mac or PC warns you before installing it (steps below);
- it may have bugs: keep a copy of important decks before you edit their charts with it;
- it is provided as is, without warranty (Apache 2.0 licence); it sends nothing anywhere unless you
  click **Help › Check for updates** or attach a report to an issue yourself.

### Install on a Mac (PowerPoint for Mac, Microsoft 365)

1. Download **`open-charts-<version>.pkg`** below and double-click it.
2. macOS says it cannot verify the developer. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings ›
   Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to open-charts, and confirm.
   (On older macOS: right-click the file › **Open**.)
3. Click through the installer (*Install for me only*).
4. Start PowerPoint. When macOS asks whether open-charts may control PowerPoint, click **OK**. If
   PowerPoint asks about macros for `open-charts.ppam`, click **Enable Macros**.

### Install on Windows (PowerPoint for Microsoft 365 or Office 2016 and newer)

1. Quit PowerPoint. Download **`open-charts-<version>.msi`** below (not the `-machine` one, which is
   for IT departments) and double-click it.
2. Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC. Click **More info › Run anyway**.
3. Start PowerPoint. No admin rights are needed.

If your company's IT blocks unsigned software or all macros, open-charts cannot run on that computer
yet; try it on a personal computer.

### Try it

On the **open-charts** tab (left of Home): pick a chart type, then **Edit data** to type your
numbers. **Help › Welcome** opens a sample deck with one chart of each type. The
[user guide](https://github.com/sunajeb/open-charts/blob/master/docs/USER-GUIDE.md) explains the rest.

### Tell us how it went

- Something broke: click **Help › Report a problem**, then attach the zip it saves to a
  [new issue](https://github.com/sunajeb/open-charts/issues/new) and say what you did.
- Anything else (what was confusing, what you missed, what you liked): open an issue or reply to the
  person who sent you this link.

### Remove it

- Mac: open **Uninstall open-charts** in the *Applications* folder of your home folder.
- Windows: **Settings › Apps › open-charts › Uninstall**.

Charts you made stay in your decks as ordinary PowerPoint shapes.
